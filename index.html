How planning applications work

Most applications are decided by council planning officers. Larger or disputed ones go to Flintshire County Council's Planning Committee, made up of councillors. The council aims to decide most applications within eight weeks.

While an application is waiting for a decision, anyone can comment on it through the council's planning website. Comments have to be about planning issues, such as traffic, flooding, design or the effect on neighbours.

Some entries are not new buildings. "Approval of details" applications sign off the conditions attached to an earlier permission, and tree applications (TPO) ask to cut or remove protected trees.
